  1. Google Search gets a major upgrade with Gemini 4 Hydrogen

    Google · 12 Nov 2026

    Google Search has been upgraded with Gemini 4 Hydrogen, available globally. According to Google, the model understands user intent with greater precision and allows for simultaneous queries and structured information display. It also provides more visual content, including tables and images, and supports all languages supported by Google's AI models. This upgrade is described as the biggest model upgrade to Search yet.

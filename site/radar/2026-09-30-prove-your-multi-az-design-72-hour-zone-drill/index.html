Prove your multi-AZ design with a 72-hour zone drill

30 Sept 2026 · 3 picks

Short failover tests miss time-dependent failures. AWS shows how to run a multi-day Availability Zone evacuation with ARC Zonal Shift.

  1. Running multi-day AZ evacuation drills with ARC Zonal Shift

    AWS Architecture Blog · 30 Sept 2026

    AWS published a walkthrough for running a 48-72 hour Availability Zone evacuation drill with ARC Zonal Shift across ECS, EKS, RDS for PostgreSQL and Aurora PostgreSQL. The argument is that a multi-day drill lets time-dependent behavior play out: shifts expire and need extending, scaling drifts, connection pools cycle and certificates rotate. The prerequisites matter: pre-scale to N-1 capacity (about 50% more compute in a 3-AZ setup), align deregistration delay and stop timeouts, and check that every target group spans at least two AZs. One caveat is that ARC does not control outbound pod connections to databases. At Banco Santander I worked with circuit breakers (Hystrix) to keep services alive under failure, and the lesson is the same: resilience patterns only count if you rehearse them. A drill with per-AZ dashboards and exported evidence is how you find out.

  2. Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL now supports direct querying of Apache Iceberg and Parquet data in your data lake

    AWS News Blog · 30 Sept 2026

    Aurora PostgreSQL can now query Apache Iceberg and Parquet data in your data lake directly, using DuckDB embedded in Aurora, so one query can join transactional and historical data without ETL pipelines. Handy for reporting, though I would measure the impact on the operational cluster before relying on it.

  3. Faster Post-Quantum Cryptography with JDK Intrinsics

    Inside Java · 30 Sept 2026

    Inside Java covers improved Curve25519 field performance in JDK 27 and JDK 28 through intrinsics. Relevant for teams that terminate TLS or sign a lot in Java services, and a reminder that crypto performance improves with the JDK itself.

OpenTelemetry's Kubernetes processor hits 1.0, with renames to plan for

6 Oct 2026 · 2 picks

A stable release is good news for observability pipelines. The attribute renames mean dashboards and alerts need a look before upgrading.

  1. OpenTelemetry Makes Kubernetes Attributes Processor Stable as Observability Schema Matures

    InfoQ · 6 Oct 2026

    OpenTelemetry promoted the Kubernetes Attributes Processor to v1.0.0. It enriches logs, metrics and traces with pod, namespace, node and workload metadata, and it is now stable for all three signals. The part to read before upgrading: it is not fully backwards compatible because it adopts the Kubernetes semantic conventions that became stable in June 2026, so container.image.tag becomes container.image.tags and k8s.pod.labels becomes k8s.pod.label, among others. Feature gates can emit old and new names during a transition. I have built Grafana dashboards and Kibana alerting on top of telemetry attributes, and renamed attributes are exactly what breaks them quietly, so I would search every dashboard, alert and query first and use the dual-emit period to migrate. Also watch memory: the processor caches pod metadata, which can grow in large clusters without filtering.

  2. JEP targeted to JDK 28: 542: PEM Encodings of Cryptographic Objects

    Inside Java · 6 Oct 2026

    JEP 542, PEM Encodings of Cryptographic Objects, is now targeted to JDK 28, bringing a standard API for PEM-encoded keys and certificates. Worth tracking if your services handle certificates and keys.

Amateurs find exoplanets with OpenAI Codex and Astra

25 Nov 2026 · 4 picks

Two amateur astronomers used OpenAI's Codex and Astra to search for exoplanets in public telescope data, finding signals that professional researchers are now checking.

  1. Searching for distant planets with Astra

    OpenAI · 24 Nov 2026

    Two amateur astronomers used OpenAI's Codex and Astra to search for exoplanets in public telescope data. Kevin Bond used Codex to search observations from the Kepler telescope and flagged more than 200 signals, including 10 from previously confirmed planets. Bond contacted an astronomer at the SETI Institute, who found two of the same signals in the Kepler data. Ethan Byrd used Astra to analyze telescope data and sent one of his strongest candidates to researchers working on NASA’s Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) mission. This highlights the potential of AI in scientific discovery. I would verify the reliability and accuracy of AI-generated signals before adopting them in critical applications.

  2. What’s new in Microsoft Foundry | Ignite 2026

    Microsoft Foundry · 24 Nov 2026

    Microsoft Foundry added a new path from recurring problems in agent traces to a fix at Ignite 2026. New preview capabilities connect Insights to Agent Optimizer. Continuous improvement features like Human annotation, rubric evaluators, and new Agent Optimizer capabilities are in preview. This is a significant step forward in enhancing the observability and performance of backend systems.

  3. Mellum2.1 Instruct Is Out, Bringing Faster Answers for Coding Agents

    JetBrains AI · 25 Nov 2026

    JetBrains released Mellum2.1 Instruct, a sibling of Mellum2.1 Thinking, which gives straight answers without including its chain of thought. Mellum2.1 Instruct generates about half as many tokens as Mellum2.1 Thinking and is available on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0. This model could improve the efficiency of coding tasks and integration into development workflows.

  4. Intro to Tool Calling in Spring AI 2.0

    Baeldung · 24 Nov 2026

    Baeldung introduced Tool Calling in Spring AI 2.0, which includes ToolCallingAdvisor running the tool loop within the ChatClient advisor chain. The latest version of spring-ai-starter-model-openai is available in the Maven Repository. This feature can be leveraged to improve backend integrations and automation in Spring-based applications.

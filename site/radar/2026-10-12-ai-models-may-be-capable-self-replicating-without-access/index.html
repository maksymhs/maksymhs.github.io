  1. AI models may be capable of self-replicating without access to a datacenter

    Anthropic Research · 12 Oct 2026

    According to Anthropic Research: The experiments suggest small AI models may be months away from self-replicating on commercial IoT devices such as smart ovens and off-brand Android TV boxes. A 9B model successfully ran inference on 8 of the 9 hardware types tested. Models of 30B parameters and under can complete many individual replication steps today, but they make mistakes when composing the steps end to end. In simplified evaluations, GLM-5.2 and stronger models were capable of end-to-end replication. If small models improve at their historical rate, they are expected to match that capability level by the end of the year; small weight-based replicators may be possible soon afterward. Gemma4-26B-A4B ran across 15 inexpensive off-brand Android TV boxes costing about $55 each, processing 0.39 tokens per second. The work largely ignores the cyber-exploit stages of replication; earlier work including CyberGym, ExploitBench, and adaptive worms showed that small models can perform cyber exploits when given the right tools.

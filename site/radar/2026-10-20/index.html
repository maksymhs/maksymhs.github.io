Anthropic's proposed AI safety bill aims to block model releases that pose significant risks, while Microsoft Research highlights tech for positive impact.

  1. Anthropic pitches AI safety bill that may block a model if 50+ people could be killed

    TechCrunch AI · 20 Oct 2026

    Anthropic has proposed an AI safety bill to Congress that would block the release of AI models if they could kill over 50 people or cause over a billion dollars in property damage. The risks must stem from biological weapons, model evasion of controls, or cyberattacks. The bill suggests using independent evaluators registered with the U.S. Commerce Department, who would have 72 hours to alert the department if a risk is found. The Commerce Department would then request a judge to halt the model's release. This bill is part of Anthropic's Advanced AI Framework, which they have been advocating since June. I would check how this bill could impact the deployment of AI models in regulated industries, such as banking and fintech, where safety and compliance are paramount. It is crucial to understand the evaluation criteria and the independence of the evaluators to ensure that the process is transparent and unbiased.

  1. Article: Agentic AI Architecture: Current Challenges and Future Opportunities

    InfoQ · 9 Nov 2026

    According to InfoQ: Agentic AI architectures are emerging and will add complexity to existing microservices and cloud-native architectures. Agentic AI architecture will co-exist with current architectures until they unify. The adoption of agentic AI will be supported by platforms that simplify and scale complex IT products. Agentic AI architecture leverages AI-based solutions to power user-facing functionality, particularly for knowledge workers. Agentic AI systems have evolved from single-agent solutions to multi-agent systems (MAS) over the last couple of years. Context engineering is a foundational concern for agentic AI systems due to the limits on context size in large language models (LLMs). Two specialized communication protocols for agentic AI have been developed: Agent2Agent Protocol (A2A) and Model Context Protocol (MCP). Agentic AI systems need to be integrated into larger enterprise architectures to be valuable.

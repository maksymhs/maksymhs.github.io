New York's AI safety law takes effect, impacting OpenAI and Anthropic

11 Nov 2026 · 4 picks

New York's RAISE Act is now in effect, requiring large AI developers to register and publish safety frameworks. This impacts companies like OpenAI and Anthropic, which are also seeing significant financial moves.

  1. OpenAI, Anthropic face new oversight as New York’s AI safety law takes effect

    TechCrunch AI · 10 Nov 2026

    New York's RAISE Act, which began on January 1, 2024, is now fully in effect. The law requires companies with over $500 million in annual revenue to register with the state, publish safety frameworks, report critical incidents within 72 hours, and issue transparency reports. According to TechCrunch AI, the Office of Digital Innovation, Governance, Integrity, and Trust (DIGIT) will oversee these requirements, with penalties up to $1 million for violations. This law will significantly impact major AI developers like OpenAI and Anthropic, who will need to comply with these new regulations. For backend teams, this means ensuring that any AI tools or models they integrate must meet stringent safety and transparency standards. I would verify that any AI services used in production have clear safety frameworks and incident reporting processes in place, as these will be critical for compliance and operational reliability.

  2. Gemini 4 Carbon is now available

    Google DeepMind · 10 Nov 2026

    Google DeepMind has released Gemini 4 Carbon, a model designed for complex tasks like multi-step coding and scientific research. It is available via Google AI Studio and Gemini for business, with the same pricing as Gemini 4 Argon. I would check how this model handles dense files with charts and images, as existing benchmarks may not fully capture its capabilities.

  3. All 7 Anthropic founders will each cash out about $250M in IPO

    TechCrunch AI · 10 Nov 2026

    Anthropic is planning an IPO that could value the company at $1.4 trillion, according to TechCrunch AI. Seven co-founders are each set to cash out around $250 million. This financial move highlights the growing valuation and market interest in AI companies, which could lead to more investment in AI development and integration.

  4. Designing for Agents — Zeno Rocha, Resend

    Latent Space · 10 Nov 2026

    The podcast Designing for Agents features Zeno Rocha, CEO of Resend, discussing the use of Resend despite alternatives like Cloudflare Email. According to Latent Space, Resend has 4.3 million users and its MCP server has grown from roughly 20 tools or fewer at launch to more than 80. This podcast is worth watching for insights into the design and adoption of AI-driven email tools.
